About NexGen

Empowering creators and brands to build meaningful connections

Our Mission

To revolutionize influencer marketing by creating a transparent, efficient, and mutually beneficial platform where brands and creators can thrive together.

We believe in the power of authentic connections and are committed to fostering genuine partnerships that deliver real value to all stakeholders.

From idea to industry leader

NexGen was founded with a vision to simplify influencer marketing and create better opportunities for creators.

2021 - Growth Phase

2026 - Industry Leader

Over 50,000 influencers and 10,000 brands trust NexGen for their influencer marketing needs.
